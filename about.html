About Us

Our Vision

To be the leading student technology community that produces confident, creative problem solvers.

Our Mission

To give every student a place to learn, practise and share technology skills through workshops, projects and teamwork.

Our Objectives

  1. Teach practical programming and web skills.
  2. Organise hackathons and competitions.
  3. Link students with industry professionals.
  4. Encourage teamwork and innovation.
