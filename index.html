Who are we?

The Tech Innovators Club is a student society for people who love computers, coding and new ideas. We meet every week to learn together, build projects and have fun.

Why join us?

  • Learn to code in a friendly group
  • Take part in hackathons and competitions
  • Meet guest speakers from industry
  • Build projects for your portfolio
